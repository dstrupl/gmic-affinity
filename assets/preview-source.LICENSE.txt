Original is a 4288x2848 JPEG photograph (Nikon D300). Downscaled to a
512px max edge (512x340) and converted to RGB TIFF for use as the fixed
input to build-time G'MIC filter previews. A real photograph is used
(rather than artwork) so previews show how each filter affects an actual
photo — skin tones, hair detail, and brick texture give varied content.
